Soul's Remnant

Soul's Remnant. A 2-D, free to play MMO that is very underated. It is a 2-D platforming based RPG, with a pixel-art aesthetic. This game has a lot of negative reviews, but don't be fooled. Those "reviews" are by people who got banned from the game for being rude. I have a lot of fond memories over this game. Everyone who I spoke to in-game was very kind, and the community is very helpful to new players.
