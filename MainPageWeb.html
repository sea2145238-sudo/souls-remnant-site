Soul's Remnant

Soul's Remnant. A 2-D, free to play MMO that is very underated.

  • The game is a 2-D platforming based RPG, with a pixel-art aesthetic.
  • Despite having a lot of negative reviews, the game is an "underdog" in my opinion.
  • Those "reviews" are by people who got banned from the game for being rude.
  • The player base is very kind, and everyone is willing to help you, and answer any questions you might have about the game.

Details about the game include:

  • Under development for 10+ years.
  • Development began April 16, 2016, under the name untitled, later changed Soul’s Remnant Online.
  • The game is primarly designed by 2 people: Chaomoon and Rajah, but the game is credited to Chaomoon with creation, as it was her idea that she developed.
  • The game had originally ran on Gamemaker Studio, however, in 2024 the game was moved to Godot 4, requireing the entire game thus far to be re-created.
